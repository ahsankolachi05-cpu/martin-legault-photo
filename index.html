The Artist

Photography as an art that evokes emotions — light, weather and a fraction of a second, held still.

Based in Ottawa, Martin Legault works across Canada and beyond: the capital in winter, Toronto's streets, Montréal at dawn, the Gaspésie coast, Ireland and the Bosphorus. His recent portrait series was made with SPAO — the School of the Photographic Arts Ottawa — in Winter 2026.
